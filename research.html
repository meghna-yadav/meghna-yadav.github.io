Working papers & works in progress

  • In progress

    Bureaucrat Tenure and Use of Local Knowledge: Evidence from Rural Development Officials in India

    Officials posted to a place for a long time accumulate knowledge about it that no administrative record holds. This paper examines how the length of a posting shapes the local knowledge rural development officials draw on, and what that means for how programs are carried out on the ground.

  • In progress

    Bureaucratic Discretion and Assignment: Evidence from Forest Rights in India

    Which official handles which case is itself a decision, taken before any claim is judged. This paper studies how discretion over assignment operates in the administration of forest rights claims in India.

  • In progress

    Party Alignment and Investment in Legibility by Local Governments: Evidence from ‘Mission Antyodaya’

    Making a population legible to the state takes effort, and that effort is political. Drawing on India's Mission Antyodaya survey, this paper asks whether local governments aligned with the party in power above them invest differently in producing information about their own residents.

  • In progress

    Downstream Effects of Women's Representation: Caste and Gender in India's Political Quotas

    India reserves seats both for women and for scheduled castes and tribes, and the two mandates can fall on the same constituency. This paper examines what follows from that overlap for representation along caste and gender.
